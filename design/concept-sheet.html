MARS PATROL

A side-scrolling rover patrol across Mars. The colony's abandoned terraforming machines are tearing up the supply road. Jump the craters, blast the boulders and drones, and beat par to every relay beacon from Chryse Flats to Olympus.

Top barScore, rovers left, current beacon and the time since the last one. Three warning lamps light before trouble arrives: AIR (machines overhead), MINE (charges on the road ahead) and REAR (a dozer behind you).
Route stripThe whole route on one line: 5 sectors of 5 beacons. The filled part is road you've covered; the marker is you.
RoadThe rover sits at the left third and slides forward when you boost, so you see more road ahead at speed. Craters and the land behind are drawn in code, in four parallax layers.
Touch layoutOn wide phones the buttons sit in the bars beside the game. On 16:9 screens they overlay the bottom corners, where the ground strip leaves room.

Your rover

Seen side-on, nose to the right. It never rotates: it tilts a pixel on landing and bounces on rough road. If a pick faces left, press Mirror.

Air threats

The terraformers' survey fleet. Only your upward shot reaches them. The AIR lamp lights a moment before each wave.

Ground threats

Things on the road. Jump them or shoot them with the forward shot, which only reaches about a third of the screen. Points for a jump are shown first, then for a kill.

Course

Craters, terrain and the four background layers are drawn in code. These two are sprites.

How a patrol plays

5 sectors × 5 beaconsChryse Flats, Ares Vallis, Tharsis Rise, Noctis Labyrinth, Olympus Approach. Each sector adds a new threat and a new backdrop.
Par timeEach sector has a par time. Clear it to score 1,000 plus 100 for every second under par. The full route adds 5,000 on top.
Respawn at the beaconLose a rover and you restart from the last beacon you passed. The sector clock keeps running.
Day, then DuskThe first run is the Day shift. Then the same route at Dusk, with faster machines, denser hazards and Mars's blue sunset. Dusk repeats and gets harder each lap.
Extra roversAt 10,000, 30,000 and 60,000 points. Start with 3.
ContinueAfter game over you can continue from the start of the sector, with the score reset to zero.

From the arcade original

Re-imagined from Irem's 1982 Moon Patrol. The mechanics are borrowed; the setting, names, art and route are new.

Kept
  • Auto-scrolling road with brake and boost, where speed changes jump length
  • One button fires forward and up at once
  • Three warning lamps, beacon checkpoints and a route strip
  • Par-time bonus per sector, and the second, harder lap
  • Air bombs that blast fresh craters into the road
Changed
  • Mars, not the Moon: rogue terraforming machines instead of aliens
  • Numbered beacons (2-3) in 5 named sectors instead of 26 lettered checkpoints
  • Beginner and Champion courses become Day and Dusk shifts
  • Respawn at the last beacon, not the last major checkpoint
New
  • Ice cores to grab at the top of a jump
  • Relay satellites that dive at you, so braking and boosting dodge as well as aim
  • Juice: dust trails, shockwaves, screen shake in game pixels
  • Controller and touch from day one
